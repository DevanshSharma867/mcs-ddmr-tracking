# Paper track: rules

Reproducibility paper on AFNTSMDC (*Nonlinear Dynamics* 2022), written in addition to the course
report. These rules apply to the paper **and** the report. This file is the committed copy of the
"Paper track" section of the local (git-ignored) CLAUDE.md; keep the two in sync.

1. **Framing.** This is a reproducibility study of AFNTSMDC (*Nonlinear Dynamics* 2022), not a
   "new controller" paper. Contributions:
   - (a) independent reproduction with a full assumption registry
   - (b) a sign error in the printed switching term, with derivation and evidence
   - (c) a discrete-time quasi-sliding diagnosis and a Ts-aware boundary-layer sizing rule
   - (d) a pre-registered ablation of an ESO-based fix, including the negative result A4 ≈ A3
   - (e) open, deterministic code
2. **Claims ledger.** Every claim in the paper or the report must appear in `docs/paper/claims.md` with:
   - the claim
   - the evidence file(s) and the command that regenerates them
   - the strength (proven / demonstrated in sim / suggested)
   - caveats

   No claim goes into the paper without an entry.
3. **Generated numbers only.** Every number in the paper is generated by a script into `results/paper/`
   (as CSV or a LaTeX macro file), never typed by hand.
4. **Evidence tiers.** Every result carries one of three tiers. The classification lives in
   `docs/paper/evidence_tiers.md`.
   - **Confirmatory:** formally pre-registered. Currently Phase 6 P1–P7; future held-out confirmation
     will also count.
   - **Pre-specified:** thresholds or predictions committed **before** the runs that test them, with the
     commit hashes showing the order. A threshold committed in the same commit as its results does not
     qualify. Going forward, commit the threshold or prediction in its own commit first.
   - **Exploratory:** everything else.
5. **Tone toward the original authors.** Factual and respectful: "We find the printed sign of term X
   is inconsistent with …", never "the authors were wrong". Present possible explanations, e.g. a
   typesetting error.
6. **No reproduction of the original paper's figures, text or tables**, beyond short, properly cited
   quotes of equations. We regenerate everything ourselves.
7. **No invented citations.** Every reference needs a verified DOI or URL, recorded in
   `docs/paper/references.bib` with a "verified" note.
8. **No irreversible or externally visible actions without explicit go-ahead.** Never perform
   irreversible or externally visible actions — history rewrites, force pushes, deleting branches or
   files, making repos public, sending emails, submitting anything — without my explicit go-ahead
   in that turn, even if credentials are available. Propose the commands and wait.
   **Standing exception (granted 2026-10-19):** normal, non-force pushes to the private `origin/main`
   are fine without asking. Everything else in this rule still needs explicit go-ahead in that turn.
